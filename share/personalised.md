### 8\. Final recommendation

For your profile, I would allocate application effort in this order:

* Funded research master's: UBC thesis MSc and several Erasmus Mundus AI/data-science programs, after checking GPA and course prerequisites.

* Fully funded doctoral routes open to bachelor's graduates: UIC and selected direct-entry PhD programs, but only after verifying the academic threshold.

* Hong Kong PhD Fellowship: A high-value option if a participating university considers your academic and research profile competitive.

* Government scholarships for the next cycle: DAAD STEM, MEXT, GKS, Stipendium Hungaricum and Türkiye Scholarships, checking the country-specific rules.

* Paid European PhD jobs: Build a longer-term list, especially for opportunities requiring a master's degree.

Your industry experience is a genuine advantage for applied AI and industry-linked research. Your biggest unknown is whether your academic record meets each program's minimum. Before applying, gather your official transcript, GPA conversion policy, IELTS score report, CV and evidence of your strongest AI projects.

One final point: a university's ranking cannot compensate for a program's funding gap. Before accepting any offer, calculate tuition, fees, insurance and realistic living expenses against the actual guaranteed funding—not an estimated scholarship or an assistantship you have not yet secured.
