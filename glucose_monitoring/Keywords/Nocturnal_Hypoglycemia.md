Nocturnal hypoglycemia is when blood sugar levels drop too low during sleep, usually below 70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L).

Common symptoms:

* Nightmares or disturbed sleep

* Night sweats

* Headache upon waking

* Feeling tired or confused in the morning

* Shaking or a fast heartbeat

Causes: Too much insulin or diabetes medication, skipping dinner, or exercising heavily before bedtime.

Prevention: Monitor blood glucose, follow your prescribed medication plan, and eat meals regularly. Severe hypoglycemia can cause seizures or unconsciousness and requires urgent medical attention.
