One additional recent paper worth exploring is [Within-person modeling of postprandial glucose using multimodal wearable data (2026)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2026.1847884/full?utm_source=chatgpt.com). It analyzes meal-related data alongside wrist movement and other wearable signals, although its main outcome is glucose dynamics rather than raw waveform changes.

My recommendation: Start with papers 1 and 2 for the physiological hypothesis, then paper 4 for the sensor-data methodology.

A key research gap to investigate is whether the shape and characteristics of raw PPG signals change consistently after glucose ingestion, independently of heart rate, movement, stress, and meal composition. These papers provide starting points, but they do not establish that glucose ingestion produces a unique, detectable smartwatch waveform.
