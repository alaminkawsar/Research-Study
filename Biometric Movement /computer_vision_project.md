### Computer vision datasets for gait analysis

For computer vision research, you should prioritize datasets containing RGB walking videos, human silhouettes, body keypoints, skeleton sequences, optical flow, and 3D body representations.

Below are the strongest datasets for training and evaluating computer vision models for gait recognition and movement analysis.

1\. CASIA-B

Best baseline benchmark

* 124 individuals

* 13,640 sequences

* RGB videos and binary silhouettes

* 11 camera viewing angles

* Normal walking, carrying bags and clothing changes

Use: CNN, GaitSet, GaitPart, gait identification.

[Dataset information](https://www.cbsr.ia.ac.cn/english/Gait%20Databases.asp?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

![北京师范大学人工智能学院](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/vJxM2iYL0ctPDnFwx6Ogd5wMVli8th4WZkqYO9uetFX8nMGKTe8iXUSkYRNBW5zC2tuTWU44jE0kw_hi4PhMcjj4gb0T5Eg_znTzWsXzbVsLv9WON2NABl-_23ReSetnzI7Mu2-WbF4xsjVYCTAiIkluhZpPVormOyh635g_s1CICdAgE3KTwOw_kkAnAASI?purpose=fullsize)

2\. GREW

Best large-scale benchmark

* 26,345 identities

* 128,671 sequences

* 882 cameras

* Real-world surveillance-style videos

* Human bounding boxes and gait representations

Use: Large-scale gait recognition, domain generalization and person identification.

[Research paper and benchmark](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/html/Zhu_Gait_Recognition_in_the_Wild_A_Benchmark_ICCV_2021_paper.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

3\. Gait3D

Best 3D computer vision option

* 4,000 identities

* More than 25,000 sequences

* 39 camera views

* 2D silhouettes and keypoints

* 3D SMPL human body representations

Use: 3D human reconstruction, gait recognition, body-shape and motion analysis.

[Official dataset website](https://gait3d.github.io/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

4\. SUSTech1K

* 1,050 individuals

* 25,239 sequences

* Real-world walking videos

* Clothing, bags, occlusion and nighttime conditions

5\. Health&Gait

Best for gait analysis

* 398 participants

* 1,564 videos

* 2D human pose estimation

* Silhouette images

* Semantic segmentation

* Optical flow

* Anthropometric and gait measurements

Use: Gait classification, walking speed estimation, pose analysis and clinical screening.

* More than 16,000 sequences

Gait recognition / movement analysis

### One question to identify the best dataset

Are you primarily trying to:

* A. Identify a person using their walking pattern (biometric identification).

* B. Detect abnormal walking, limping or gait disorders.

* C. Estimate walking speed, stride length, step length and joint angles.

* D. Recognize human movements and activities from video.

* E. Develop a complete AI-based gait analysis system.

I can then narrow the research to datasets with actual computer vision files, direct download access, annotation formats and suitable training benchmarks.
